Pricing & Packaging Specification (PPS)
1. Purpose
This document defines the commercial packaging structure for TELLANN.
The Pricing & Packaging Specification establishes:
* Subscription plans
* Included capabilities
* Plan limits
* Feature entitlements
* Commercial upgrade paths
* Packaging evolution strategy
This document serves as the authoritative source for all customer-facing plans.
2. Packaging Philosophy
TELLANN pricing is designed around customer value.
Customers purchase:
* Behavioral visibility
* Workflow understanding
* Quality confidence
Customers do not purchase:
* Events
* Logs
* Traces
* Internal compute
During Phase 1, packaging shall be based primarily on:
* Applications
* Users
* Storage
* Retention

The Tellann desktop agent is free to download and is available on every plan, including Free. A customer cannot demonstrate an application without it, so gating the agent itself would gate the product. What plans differ on is how much the agent is allowed to do — specifically whether it may analyze the codebase, draft flows from documents, and instrument automatically.

Automated Instrumentation is the clearest upgrade trigger. Without it a developer instruments by hand, following the plan the agent shows them, and the demonstration proceeds normally. With it the agent applies and rolls back the change itself. Free and Local customers get a complete product; paid customers get their time back.

API Access means scoped programmatic access to management, reporting, graph, coverage, and export APIs. SDK ingestion credentials are available on every plan within the applicable credential limit.

Capability tiers are defined in the Entitlement Specification, Section 6.

14. Packaging Governance
Any change to:
* Plan pricing
* Application limits
* User limits
* Storage limits
* Retention limits
* Included features
must be approved through formal pricing review.
Packaging changes must remain consistent with the Pricing Strategy Document.


15. Success Criteria
The Pricing & Packaging Model shall be considered successful when:
1. Customers can select a plan in under five minutes.
2. Plan differences are immediately understandable.
3. Upgrades occur naturally as organizations grow.
4. Pricing remains aligned with customer value.
5. Packaging supports all Phase 1 commercial objectives.
6. Future Phase 2 and Phase 3 monetization can be added without restructuring existing plans.
