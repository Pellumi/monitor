Billing & Subscription Specification (BSS)
1. Purpose
This document defines how subscriptions, billing, invoicing, payments, renewals, upgrades, downgrades, plan enforcement, taxation, and subscription lifecycle management operate within TELLANN.
The Billing & Subscription Specification establishes:
* Subscription models
* Billing rules
* Payment processing
* Currency support
* Invoice generation
* Subscription lifecycle management
* Upgrade and downgrade policies
* Overage billing framework
* Enterprise billing procedures
This document is the authoritative source for all commercial billing behavior.
2. Billing Philosophy
TELLANN bills customers based on value received.
Phase 1 customers purchase:
* Applications
* User seats
* Storage
* Quality visibility
Customers do not purchase:
* Events
* Logs
* Traces
* Internal platform compute
Billing shall remain simple, transparent, and predictable.
No hidden billing mechanisms are permitted.
To support local adoption in Nigeria, TELLANN provides a Nigeria-only Local plan with reduced limits and localized pricing.

7. Supported Payment Currencies
Primary:
* USD
* NGN
Future:
* EUR
* GBP
* CAD
* AUD
Billing identity is user-scoped. The paying user holds one billing profile —
ISO billing country, legal name, billing email, tax identifiers — and that
profile determines the billing currency, the eligible plans, and the permitted
payment processors for every organization that user pays for.
Billing currency is derived from the payer's billing country at subscription
creation and becomes the invoice currency.
The Local plan is restricted to NGN billing only.
Organizations remain the entitlement boundary: applications, seats, storage,
and retention are consumed per organization, and a subscription grants those
entitlements to exactly one organization.

choose either Nigerian processor at checkout; Paystack is selected by default.
Maintaining a single recurring-billing integration per currency is what allows
trials, prorated plan changes, and the grace period to behave identically for
every customer regardless of what they pay in.

10. Trial Period
Default Trial:
14 Days
Trial Plan:
Solo, or Local when the payer's billing country is Nigeria.
Flow:
1. A new account completes its billing profile (country and contact details).
2. The payer authorizes a card. A nominal amount is authorized to prove the card
   is chargeable; the subscription itself is not charged.
3. On confirmation from the processor, the trial starts and the account receives
   full plan entitlements immediately.
4. Nothing is billed for 14 days. A reminder is sent before the trial converts.
5. On day 14 the first full charge is taken against the stored card, and the
   subscription becomes an ordinary paid subscription.
6. Cancelling or changing plan before day 14 means no charge is ever taken.
A trial cannot begin without a chargeable card on file, because a trial that
cannot convert is a trial that silently fails.
Free does not require a trial because it remains available without time limit. Enterprise trials are negotiated and are not activated automatically.
Restrictions:
* No multiple concurrent trials
* One trial per organization
* One trial per paying user, so a payer cannot re-trial through a new organization
11. Upgrade Policy
Upgrades take effect immediately.
Billing is prorated.
The prorated difference is charged against the payment method already on file,
on whichever processor holds it. The customer is not sent back through checkout.
Example:
Upgrade:
Local → Solo
Remaining Days:
15
Customer charged:
Difference × Remaining Billing Period
12. Downgrade Policy
Downgrades become effective at the next renewal.
Reason:
Prevent accidental data loss.
Nothing is charged at the time a downgrade is scheduled; the new, lower price
simply applies from the next billing date.
Customer receives warnings if new plan limits are exceeded.
13. Renewal Policy
Monthly Plans
Renew every 30 days.
Annual Plans
Renew every 365 days.
Automatic renewal enabled by default.
14. Failed Payment Handling
A renewal that does not succeed on its due date starts a grace period
immediately. The customer keeps full plan access for the entire window.
Grace Period Duration:
7 Days
During Grace Period:
* Full plan entitlements remain active
* The stored payment method is retried once per day
* The customer is emailed at the start of the window with the deadline
* Updating the card triggers an immediate retry
After Grace Period:
The subscription is scaled down to the Free plan.
Downgrading rather than suspending is deliberate: an expired card is the common
case, and cutting a paying customer off entirely punishes an administrative
lapse. The plan held before the lapse is recorded so resubscribing offers it
back directly.
15. Reduced Access Behavior
An organization scaled down to Free is subject to Free plan limits. Data is
retained under the Free retention window; nothing is deleted at the moment of
downgrade.
Suspension remains available for accounts suspended for cause rather than
non-payment. Suspended organizations:
Allowed:
* View Existing Reports
* Export Existing Data
Blocked:
* New Demonstrations
* New Session Recording
* New Application Creation
* New Report Generation

17. Taxation Framework
Tax is calculated by Tellann from the paying user's ISO billing country and is
applied before the customer is charged.
Published prices are tax-inclusive. The price shown in the Pricing & Packaging
Specification is the total the customer pays; the tax component is extracted
from it and stated separately on the invoice. Charging tax on top of a published
price changes what the customer pays and is therefore a pricing change requiring
review under PPS §14.
Registered jurisdictions:
* NG — VAT at 7.5%
A country outside the registered list is charged no tax, and the invoice states
that explicitly rather than omitting the line.
Every invoice and receipt must clearly display:
* Tax Amount
* Tax Rate
* Tax Jurisdiction
18. Customer Billing Portal
The paying user shall access:
* Current Plan
* Next Renewal Date
* Trial Status And First Charge Date
* Usage Summary
* Payment Method
* Billing History
* Download Invoices And Receipts
* Upgrade Plan
* Downgrade Plan
* Cancel Subscription
Every invoice is downloadable as a PDF from the billing page and is also emailed
to the organization's billing contacts with the PDF attached. An unpaid invoice
downloads as an invoice; once settled the same record downloads as a receipt.
Upgrades and downgrades are available to every customer on every processor and
in every supported currency.

22. Subscription Governance
Billing changes must:
* Preserve transparency
* Preserve customer trust
* Remain aligned with PSD
* Remain aligned with PPS
* Be auditable
* Be documented
No hidden fees are permitted.
No undocumented overages are permitted.
23. Success Criteria
The Billing System shall be considered successful when:
1. Customers understand billing immediately.
2. Upgrades are frictionless.
3. Billing remains predictable.
4. Phase 1 remains application-based.
5. Phase 2 event billing can be introduced without redesign.
6. Phase 3 AI billing can be introduced without redesign.
7. Enterprise billing is supported.
8. USD and NGN customers can subscribe seamlessly.
9. Nigerian startups and individual developers can access an affordable entry-level paid plan through the Local plan.
