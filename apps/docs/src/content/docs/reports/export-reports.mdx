# Export Reports

Learn how export reports works within Reports and how to apply it safely in Tellann.

## Purpose

**Export Reports** turns shareable interpretations of graphs, coverage, gaps, sessions, and endpoint evidence into a reviewable artifact for engineering, QA, product, or administrative decisions. Each conclusion should remain traceable to a graph, workflow, session, endpoint, or coverage observation.

## When to use it

Use this report when its evidence matches the decision being made. Executive summaries communicate risk and trend; flow, graph, gap, session, and endpoint reports support deeper diagnosis.

## Included evidence

The report includes the applicable scope, time window, environment, processing status, source artifacts, and findings. Retention and plan entitlements can limit the historical evidence available to regenerate it.

## Metrics and interpretation

Interpret percentages with their denominators and collection conditions. High observed coverage does not prove correctness, and a missing path can indicate either a real quality gap or behavior that was outside the observed scenario.

## Filters and exports

Apply environment, application, workflow, session, or time filters before export. Exported data should preserve labels and provenance so reviewers can reproduce the view without receiving product credentials.

## Limitations and related reports

Reports summarize observed evidence; they do not replace assertions, security review, accessibility testing, or human validation. Compare adjacent report types when a single metric cannot explain the underlying behavior.
